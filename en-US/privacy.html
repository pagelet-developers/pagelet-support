Privacy Policy

Operator Yifei You · Support and deletion requests contact_felix_you@163.com

Effective 2026-11-17

Pagelet stores local copies of imported materials, extracted text, generated learning pages, exercises, answers, reading positions, and settings on your Mac. API keys are stored in the system Keychain. The app currently has no developer-operated account service, advertising or behavioral analytics SDK, or cross-device synchronization.

When you choose generation or other model-assisted processing, the app sends the selected text, necessary PDF page images, task instructions and parameters to the model service you configure, using your API key for authentication. Outline processing may send table-of-contents or candidate content pages and headings. Page questions send the current explanation, relevant source text and your question. Connection tests send a small test request. The provider also receives ordinary network request metadata, such as your IP address. Your materials may contain personal information; send only content you are authorized to use.

The developer does not operate a central model proxy. Your provider's retention, training, pricing, and deletion policies apply. Pagelet cannot promise that an arbitrary provider does not retain requests or use them for training. Review your provider's policy before using its service.

Reading and practicing saved content offline does not require new model requests. Avoiding online generation prevents new task uploads. Stopping a task cancels further processing and discards its results, but cannot recall requests already received by a provider.

Removing a textbook from the library deletes its associated local content and study records, without deleting the original imported file. Clearing a service's API key in Settings and saving removes its local Keychain entry. Local deletion does not delete records retained by a provider; contact that provider for deletion. Uninstalling the app may leave local data or Keychain entries behind.

If you contact support, the developer receives the messages and diagnostic information you choose to provide, solely to handle your request. Do not send API keys or material you lack permission to share.

GitHub Pages hosts the support and privacy pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The pages contain no analytics scripts or data collection forms.

Support emails and necessary attachments are generally retained for 90 days after the case is closed. We retain them longer when required by law.
