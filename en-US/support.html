Open the built-in sample from the library to try short reading pages and exercises. After importing your own material, choose Read feed or Practice directly in the book. At the end of a passage, you can practice that passage or keep reading.

To generate new content, configure a compatible model service and your API key in Settings. PDF contents detection, explanations and exercises may require image input. Review the destination and data shown before sending. Your key is stored in Keychain and any model charges come from the provider you choose.

When contacting support, tell us your Pagelet and macOS versions, the error message and the steps that reproduce it. Remove keys and personal material from any screenshots. Privacy and data use is available in Settings. Remove a book from the library to delete its study records.
